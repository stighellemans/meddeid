# Team, funding and citation

## Organizations behind MedDeID

MedDeID is developed through a clinical–academic collaboration at the
University of Antwerp and Antwerp University Hospital (UZA). The work brings
together data science and software engineering from **Adrem Data Lab**, clinical
data infrastructure and technical expertise from the **UZA Data Innovation
Lab**, and research support from the **Research Foundation – Flanders (FWO)**.

## How to cite MedDeID

Once the MedDeID article has a DOI, cite the article for the overall framework.
Also cite the exact software, model or dataset release used so the work can be
reproduced.

Until then, use this project-level citation and replace the bracketed fields:

> Hellemans, S., Stroobants, T., Scheurwegs, E., Meysman, P., Jorens, P., &
> Laukens, K. (2026). *MedDeID for locally deployable clinical text
> de-identification with real or synthetic training data* [Software]. Version
> [version]. [repository or DOI].

For a reproducible citation, include:

- the component name and version;
- the model repository and immutable revision;
- the dataset split and immutable revision or DOI;
- the annotation-guideline version, when relevant.

The [MedDeID collection on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/collections/stighellemans/meddeid)
links to the current model and dataset cards. Prefer an artifact's own citation
block when available.

## Licence

MedDeID software repositories use `AGPL-3.0-only` unless stated otherwise.
Each repository's `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files are authoritative.

Models, datasets, annotation guidelines and lookup resources may use different
terms. Check the licence and attribution information attached to each artifact
before redistribution. External comparison systems retain their own licences.
